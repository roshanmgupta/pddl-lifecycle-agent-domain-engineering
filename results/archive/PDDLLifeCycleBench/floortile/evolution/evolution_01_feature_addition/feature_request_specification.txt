- changing the color of a painting robot: (change-color ?r - robot ?c - color)

- moving the robot up, down, right, and left across clear adjacent tiles:
  (up ?r - robot ?x - tile ?y - tile)
  (down ?r - robot ?x - tile ?y - tile)
  (right ?r - robot ?x - tile ?y - tile)
  (left ?r - robot ?x - tile ?y - tile)
