REQUIREMENT SPECIFICATION (AUTOPLANBENCH / CLASSICAL PLANNING GROUNDING):
Extend the existing operational model of the rovers domain to incorporate direct orbital telemetry relay for scientific rock data.

The baseline domain enables:
- navigating a rover between waypoints: (navigate ?x - rover ?y - waypoint ?z - waypoint)
- sampling soil and rock samples into stores: (sample_soil ...), (sample_rock ...)
- dropping sample store contents: (drop ?x - rover ?y - store)
- calibrating cameras and taking multi-mode images: (calibrate ...), (take_image ...)
- communicating scientific data through a line-of-sight lander with a free communication channel:
  (communicate_soil_data ?r - rover ?l - lander ?p - waypoint ?x - waypoint ?y - waypoint)
  (communicate_rock_data ?r - rover ?l - lander ?p - waypoint ?x - waypoint ?y - waypoint)
  (communicate_image_data ?r - rover ?l - lander ?o - objective ?m - mode ?x - waypoint ?y - waypoint)
