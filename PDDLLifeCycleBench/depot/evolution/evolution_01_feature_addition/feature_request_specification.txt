================================================================================
CATEGORY C: CONTINUOUS DOMAIN EVOLUTION SPECIFICATION (CAPABILITY EXTENSION)
BASELINE DOMAIN: DEPOT -> EVOLVED DOMAIN: DEPOT (DIRECT CROSS-DOCKING)
================================================================================
REQUIREMENT SPECIFICATION (AUTOPLANBENCH NATURAL LANGUAGE ENCODING):
Extend the existing operational model of the depot domain to support direct vehicle-to-vehicle cross-docking operations.

I can carry out the following new action:
cross load a crate B with a hoist A from a truck C into a truck D at a place E

I have the following restrictions on my actions:
I can only cross load a crate B with a hoist A from a truck C into a truck D at a place E if it is the case that A is at E and A is available and C is at E and D is at E and B is in C

The actions have the following effects on the state:
Once I cross load a crate B with a hoist A from a truck C into a truck D at a place E, it is the case that B is in D and A is available
Once I cross load a crate B with a hoist A from a truck C into a truck D at a place E, it is not the case anymore that B is in C

SOLVABILITY PRESERVATION MANDATE:
All baseline actions (driving a truck, lifting a crate, dropping a crate, loading a crate, and unloading a crate) must remain intact so that all baseline benchmark problem instances remain 100% solvable without reachability regressions.
