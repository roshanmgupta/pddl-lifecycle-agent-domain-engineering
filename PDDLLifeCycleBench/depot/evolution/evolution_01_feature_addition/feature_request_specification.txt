================================================================================
CATEGORY C: CONTINUOUS DOMAIN EVOLUTION SPECIFICATION (CAPABILITY EXTENSION)
BASELINE DOMAIN: DEPOT -> EVOLVED DOMAIN: DEPOT (DIRECT CROSS-DOCKING)
================================================================================
REQUIREMENT SPECIFICATION (AUTOPLANBENCH / CLASSICAL PLANNING GROUNDING):
Extend the existing operational model of the depot domain to support direct vehicle-to-vehicle cross-docking operations.

The baseline domain enables:
- driving a truck from one place to another place
- a hoist lifting a crate from a surface (pallet or crate) at a place
- a hoist dropping a crate onto a clear surface at a place
- a hoist loading a held crate into a truck at a place
- a hoist unloading a crate from a truck at a place

CAPABILITY EXTENSION REQUIREMENTS:
1. New Action Schema:
   - Name: cross_load (or cross-load)
   - Parameters:
     ?h - hoist, ?c - crate, ?from_truck - truck, ?to_truck - truck, ?p - place
   - Preconditions:
     The hoist ?h must be at place ?p and available. The source truck ?from_truck must be at place ?p. The destination truck ?to_truck must be at place ?p. The crate ?c must currently be in the source truck: (in ?c ?from_truck).
   - Effects:
     The crate ?c is loaded directly into the destination truck: (in ?c ?to_truck).
     The crate ?c is no longer in the source truck: (not (in ?c ?from_truck)).
     The hoist ?h remains available: (available ?h).

SOLVABILITY PRESERVATION MANDATE:
All baseline action schemas (Drive, Lift, Drop, Load, Unload) must remain intact so that all baseline benchmark problem instances remain 100% solvable without reachability regressions.
